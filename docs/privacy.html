Privacy Policy

Duo Slot — September 30, 2026

Duo Slot does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Duo Slot does not connect to the internet. Everything it reads stays on your device, and the developer cannot see it.

What the app reads, and why

  • Steps (Health and Motion) — to show today’s steps on your watch face. Read only; nothing is written to Health.
  • Calendar — to show your current or next event on your watch face. Read only.
  • Battery level — to show your Apple Watch battery on your watch face.

You can decline any of these. Items you decline simply show “No access” or “--”.

What is stored on the device

The last values read (steps, battery level, upcoming events) are kept on your Apple Watch so the complication can show them. They are never sent anywhere and are deleted when you delete the app.

In-app purchases

None. Duo Slot is free.

Sharing with third parties

None.
